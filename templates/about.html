About Veda AI

Veda AI is a minimalistic, high-performance Video Lecture Analyzer designed to convert lengthy, unstructured YouTube educational videos into concise, highly structured teacher notes.

By leveraging advanced Large Language Models like NVIDIA NIM (Nemotron) and Groq LLaMA-3, it extracts key concepts, analogies, definitions, and real-world applications in seconds. Built with a pure monochrome black and white design aesthetic inspired by MetaCleaner and Portfolio, it offers a distraction-free, lightning-fast study environment.

Technology Stack

Python Flask NVIDIA NIM (Nemotron) Groq LLaMA-3 Vanilla CSS & JS YouTube Transcript API Marked.js
