About Veda AI

Veda AI is a futuristic Video Lecture Analyzer designed to convert long, unstructured YouTube educational videos into highly structured, comprehensive teacher notes.

By leveraging advanced Large Language Models like Sarvam AI (specifically optimized for high-quality instruction extraction) and Groq LLaMA-3, it instantly provides you with point-by-point concepts, analogies, and detailed breakdowns of any lecture. The immersive 3D interface is built using Three.js and Vanta.js to provide an engaging, premium AI-OS aesthetic.
