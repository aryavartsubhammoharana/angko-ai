About Angkor AI

Angkor AI is a minimalistic, high-performance Video Intelligence Engine designed to break down YouTube videos into raw truth and actionable crux.

Instead of generic summaries, it extracts the actual ground reality: what's really happening, what's not (dispelling misconceptions and clickbait), and delivers a high-impact breakdown strictly in clear English. Powered by NVIDIA NIM, Gemini, Groq, and KaTeX math rendering with a pure monochrome B&W aesthetic inspired by MetaCleaner and Portfolio.

Technology Stack

Python Flask NVIDIA NIM (Nemotron) Groq LLaMA-3 Vanilla CSS & JS YouTube Transcript API Marked.js KaTeX Math
